The Process

Signal to insight in milliseconds.

A four-stage pipeline that turns raw behavioural data into precision advertising decisions at bid time.

01

Data Ingestion

Smart Signals ingests first-party, contextual, and behavioural signals from your properties at scale — no cookies required.

02

AI Classification

Our ML models classify intent, sentiment, and attention in real time — producing a rich signal profile for each user session.

03

Bid-Time Activation

Signal scores are delivered at bid time via our low-latency API — enabling DSPs and buyers to act on intent the moment it's detected.

04

Measurement & Loop

Post-campaign signal analytics feed back into the model — continuously improving accuracy, reducing waste, and maximising ROI.
